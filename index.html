About

The shape of the work

I started as a freelance full-stack developer in Lisbon in 2008, shipping everything from Flash microsites to payment integrations for clients who ranged from solo founders to corporate giants. That range never really went away — I've since been the person writing the architecture and the person setting the go-to-market strategy, often in the same quarter.

I've relocated to Tokyo twice: first with Hivelocity, then years later as CTO of Heartcore, where I recruited and led an engineering team, chose Rust for a new enterprise product where performance and memory safety mattered, and managed Gartner analyst relations on the side. Working from Lisbon, I co-founded Crably, a multi-tenant CMS headquartered in Denmark that I built solo from zero to customers in ten-plus countries. As CTO of Colmena, I architected an HR and benefits platform from scratch and stayed through its later acquisition by Coverflex; I also spent time on Capdesk's Denmark-based team, building a zero-to-one VC platform module on Rails.

Most recently, working remotely from Lisbon for Publitas (Netherlands), I've been back in the code full-time, benchmarking Rails and Rust APIs while driving engineering-wide improvements in testing and build reliability. The work sits at the intersection of technical depth and organizational leverage: solving problems in the stack while establishing practices that scale across the engineering team. Close enough to strategy to ask why, close enough to the stack to ship the answer.
